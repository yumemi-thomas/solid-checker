# Verified retained certification floor

A generated proposal is not an accepted coverage baseline. In the retained
Solid 1 investigation, two unproved generated cases prevented graph recovery
from reaching ten missing web cases even though ordinary certification had
accepted the other 29 cases.

For a fresh destination only, after the whole graph and retained graph baseline
refuse, run ordinary proposal certification into a private destination. Require
an exact partition of positively accepted cases and explicit certifier refusals.
Follow the resulting publication pointer and named catalogs, checking content
digests and exact package, artifact, importer, conditions and resolution inputs.
Malformed or incomplete publications are errors, not permission to omit cases.

Every accepted retained case is mandatory in the next graph union. Trial
receipts are never supplied as graph evidence: final publication independently
proves the complete selected union and authenticates its dependencies and trust.
If that accepted baseline fails in graph context, retain the ordinary fallback.
Existing destinations keep their previous protection. The original expected
census and the 32 missing-case / 1024 total-case budgets remain unchanged.

The CLI tests cover exact partitioning, foreign and duplicate selections,
missing refusals, corrupted publication references and digests, altered artifact
or importer identities, preservation of the accepted baseline, and protection
of pre-existing publications. Real-package gains require a separate measurement
of published cases and ordinary consumer verification; this decision does not
itself claim new certification or a denominator correction.
