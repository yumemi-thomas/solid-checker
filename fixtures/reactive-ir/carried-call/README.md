# carried-call

Claim (ADR 0894, review D23): a setter or accessor handed whole to a carrier that provably runs it is judged as a call of it at the carrier, with the carrier's role: SC2001 or SC1001 at the run, none in a handler. `untrack` only with a non-empty string-literal label. `UntrackInApply`: an effect apply warns too (Chrome rc.21). Negatives: `SecondArgument`, `ReduceInitial`, `ArrayFromSource`, `UntrackSecond` (not the inline position), `EmptyReceiver` (no run), `UntrackPlain` (no window), `UntrackVariableLabel`.
