Controls: `effect-cleanup-disposal-handler-escaped` (the root also handed to a helper) and `effect-cleanup-disposal-reachable` (called in the body) stay uncertifiable. Chrome rc.21 is quiet at the read after the click (ADR 1214's grid programs `effect-cleanup-dispose`). Stub: `effect-cleanup-disposal-only`'s.
