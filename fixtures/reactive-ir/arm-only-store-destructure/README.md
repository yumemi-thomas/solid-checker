A store's destructure claims the strict-read window, as SC1001 does, so it holds only where a client build runs it (ADR 1158, after ADR 0677). `ServerTag`, `ServerQuoted`, `ServerNested`, `ServerThenFirst` and `NamespaceTag` (through `web.isServer`) are rendered only under `isServer`: proved silent. `BothArms` is also rendered outside the arm and keeps its violation; `ShadowedTag` sits under a local `isServer`, no build constant, and keeps it. `UnderConstant` is uncertifiable, blocker `build-constant-arm`. `ServerProps` is a props destructure, a pattern claim of any build: it stands in the arm.
