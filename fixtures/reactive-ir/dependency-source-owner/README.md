# Dependency source: owner requirements at the project's call (ADR 0290)

An owner requirement inside a package analyzed as source (`dep-timer`) is reported at the project's call that runs it, under that call's owner context (ADR 0275/0280; not in the package's own copy, ADR 0284): the operation and every call on the way must be on its function's every-completion path, or the call's arguments decide the branch. Numeric delays, arrow delays and signal getters (a getter is a function, ADR 0338) in `onClick`, `choice(false)` and after-`await` in `try` are violations; bodies, `bind`/`length`, scheduled references clean; `useMaybe`, shared helpers, owner guards, `catch`, after-await helpers uncertifiable. In `Joint.tsx`, `useListener` (an `if`/`else` of `createEffect` and `createRenderEffect` on package state) is one joint obligation: a violation in `onClick`, while its early return, `async` and `try` variants and an effect/cleanup pair stay uncertifiable and a component-body call is clean. Stub: published 2.0.0-rc.13 signatures.
