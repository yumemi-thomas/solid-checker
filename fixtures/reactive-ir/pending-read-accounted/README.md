# pending-read-accounted

Claim (ADR 0838): every read of a source not proved synchronous is accounted for, per pending rule (SC5001, SC5003, SC5008): a row of that rule at the read (or one whose `origin`/`pending_origin` is the read), an SC9012 at the read, or a recorded reason ruling that rule out. Otherwise an uncertifiable, blocker `async-read-unaccounted`. Positives (walk ends with no row): `TwoReadsOneCall` (one row per call), `RewrittenHelper` (written binding), `GeneratorHelper` (generator helper), `MixedBoundPath` and `TwoCallers` (review R1/R6: a placed call's row speaks for its own path; a cut path leaves the read its own rows, ADR 0835), `BareProbe` and `HelperBareProbe` (review R18: a probe with no `Loading`, Chrome rc.21 warns), `ExportedHelper` and `ShorthandReference` (review R17, D9: a caller the walk cannot see), `EscapedComponentFlush` (R2h: a body role of an escaped component, one returning JSX and also handed to `forEach`, is no reason; Chrome rc.21 warns), `StoreKeysCaught` (R2h: a pending store root handed on whole, `Object.keys(store)`, is a site) and `GetterCaught` (R2h: a getter run by `held.v` in a `try`, the caught claim stays open). Negatives, no pending row: `HandlerRead`, `CoveredRead`, `ProbeRead`, `ComputationRead`, and the walk-decided `HandlerHelper`, `TimerHelper`, `CoveredProbeHelper`. Stubs as in `handed-closure-pending-read`, plus `Loading` and `isPending` from rc.21.
