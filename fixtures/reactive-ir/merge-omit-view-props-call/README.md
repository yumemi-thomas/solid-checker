# merge-omit-view-props-call

**Claim (ADR 1100).** A call of a member of a `const` `merge` or `omit` view is the call of the props parameter's own member when the view's rc.21 Get is the parameter's Get: `merge(literal…, props)` with the parameter last and each earlier source a closed object literal without the key (the key no member of `Object.prototype`), or `omit(props, "k"…)` with string-literal keys other than it. `MergeRead` and `OmitRead`, a tracked read of the accessor every caller passes, are proved. `MergeBody` and `OmitBody`, the same reads untracked in the component body, keep their violations. `ParameterFirst`, `UnknownSource`, `OmitPredicate`, `Computed`, `Nested` and `Shadowed` stay uncertifiable.
